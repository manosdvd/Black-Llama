Recommended Gear Packing List

🎒 Personal Gear

  • BSA Field Uniform (Class A) & Activity T-Shirts (Class B)
  • Warm sleeping bag (rated to 40°F) & sleeping pad
  • Sturdy hiking boots (broken in) & extra socks
  • Warm jacket / fleece (Mt. Lemmon nights are cold!)
  • Flashlight / headlamp with spare batteries
  • Water bottle (minimum 2 liters capacity)
  • Sunscreen, insect repellent, and lip balm
  • Personal toiletries & towel

⛺ Troop Equipment

  • Troop first aid kit & medical records (A, B, C)
  • Fire buckets, rake, shovel for campsite safety
  • Dutch ovens, charcoal, and cooking chimney
  • Lanterns (battery-powered preferred)
  • BSA and Troop Flags
  • Camp chairs for adult leaders

Camp Rules & Forest Mandates

🔥 Fire Safety Policy

All open fires must be confined to the designated steel rings in your campsite. A 10-foot clearing around the ring is mandatory. Keep a filled water bucket and shovel nearby. Do not leave fires unattended. Restrictions change daily with USFS directives.

🐻 Wildlife Protocols

Camp Lawton sits in bear and coati habitat. No food, candy, toothpaste, or scented items are permitted in tents. Store all foodstuffs in campsite bear boxes or the dining hall. Pack trash out to dumpsters daily.

💧 Hydration & Altitude

At 7,779 feet elevation, dehydration occurs rapidly. All campers must drink at least 3-4 liters of water daily. Monitor scouts for signs of altitude sickness (headache, fatigue, nausea).

📙 2026 Camp Staff Handbook

Welcome to the Camp Lawton Staff Association

Being on staff at Camp Lawton is both a privilege and a high responsibility. You represent Scouting America and the Catalina Council to hundreds of youth weekly.

1. Code of Professional Conduct

All staff must adhere to the Scout Oath and Scout Law. Professional relationships must be maintained at all times. Staff will follow the Youth Protection guidelines strictly (no one-on-one contact, separate housing by age groups, etc.).

2. Uniform Guidelines

- Field Uniform (Class A): Worn for morning and evening flag ceremonies, dinners, and campfire programs. Consists of official scout shirt, scout shorts/pants, scout belt, and scout socks.
- Activity Uniform (Class B): Worn during the day for teaching blocks. Consists of a Camp Lawton staff t-shirt and appropriate shorts/pants.

3. Cabin Housing & Dining Hall Rules

Staff cabins are grouped by age (under 18 Jr. Staff vs. 18+ Adult Staff). Cabins must be kept clean and are subject to inspection by the Camp Commissioner. Meals are provided in the Dining Hall. Staff must arrive 10 minutes prior to meals to help set up tables.

4. Time Off & Time Out Policy

Staff receive 24 hours of time off per week, starting Saturday at noon until Sunday at noon. All staff must check out with the Camp Director before leaving Coronado National Forest.
