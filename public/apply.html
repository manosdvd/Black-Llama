SECTION IV: Essential Functions, Environmental realities, & Life-Safety Certifications

Camp Lawton is located on Mount Lemmon at an elevation of 8,000 feet. The environment is rugged, resources are heavily conserved, and wildlife is active. Please initial in each box to acknowledge these realities.

Req.
High-Altitude & Terrain: I understand that working at Camp Lawton requires physical exertion at 8,000 feet of elevation and navigation of uneven and rugged terrain. Reasonable accommodations will be made to aid you as needed, but the reality of the camp environment is unavoidable.
Req.
Wildlife & "Smellables" Policy: I understand that wildlife such as black bears, rattlesnakes, turkeys, mountain lions, and other critters are a possibility in the camp. I agree to strictly adhere to the camp's wildlife protocols.
Req.
Water Conservation & Sanitation: I acknowledge that water is a scarce resource at camp. I agree to abide by shower time limits (3-5 minutes unless otherwise informed). I understand that my duties will include the daily cleaning and sanitation of shared facilities, including latrines (KYBOs) and shower houses.
Req.
